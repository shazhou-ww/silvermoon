# Code of Conduct

Silvermoon welcomes respectful participation from people of every background
and level of experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Respect different viewpoints, identities, and experiences.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope And Enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
spaces used to represent the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/shazhou-ww). Maintainers will
review reports in good faith and may edit or remove content, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.
